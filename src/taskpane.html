Without a key, p3n gives you 10 calls an hour, up to 5,000 characters each. For more, sign up (you get free credit), create a key on the API keys page and paste it here.

Saved as soon as p3n accepts it.

What p3n sends

Only the text you choose, and only when you click Check or Rewrite. It goes to api.p3n.si and nowhere else. The pane looks at your selection only to count its words, keeps no history and has no analytics.

Your key stays on this device, in the add-in's own storage. It's never saved inside a document or workbook, so it doesn't travel with a file you share.
