# Changelog

## 1.2.4

- In Word, a Check of text from the document marks the sentences it lists there: red for the sentences, berry for the pattern hits' sentences. They're Word's annotations, which Word draws over the text and doesn't save: nothing is written into the document. They go with the next Check or Rewrite, an edit to that text, or Clear highlights under the result. A sentence that isn't in the text checked stays in the list only. Annotations need Word with WordApi 1.7 and, Microsoft says, a Microsoft 365 subscription; other versions of Word, and Excel, show the list only.
- A link to p3n.si at the foot of the pane, under the free calls or your balance, with or without a key. It opens in your browser, not in the pane.
- The first button of the Office, Light and Dark switch at the foot is named "Match Office theme": it follows Office's theme (File > Account > Office Theme), not Windows'. In the settings, Office keeps its label and has the same tooltip.

Earlier versions are in the [git log](https://github.com/Barabosik/p3n-office-addin/commits/main).
